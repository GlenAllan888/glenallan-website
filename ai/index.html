AI

Welcome to the few things that have actually spent the time putting down as songs. Something I wish I had spent a lot more time doing. These also span 30 years. A bit longer actually, 36 years maybe.

Covers a lot of styles, but it's all, like everything else, kind of heavy and dark. I'm not a fan of music that's in a major key, which, for all intents and purposes, means happy. I like mournful, soulful, dark, industrial, electronic, EDM, glitch, dubstep, trip-hop, downtempo, and some stuff from a number of other genres as well.

But what I create, not sure what any of it is genre-wise. Probably a bit dark industrial sometimes, sometimes a little glitchy and trip-hoppy, sometimes kind of a strange electronic dirge. But I think I have a curious knack for audio as well. So, kick back, take a listen, see what you think.

3D Worlds

 

This is an interesting collection. All of these were made in the 90s, using Bryce 3D through 4D and various versions of Ray Dream Designer, two programs that ultimately, basically went away, which is kind of sad.

Ray Dream Designer I really got for some reason, and most of the time when I've tried to look at new three-dimensional software for building things, it seems to have way steeper learning curve. I do really well at learning new systems, and I haven't really given it as much of a try as I could, so I probably should. Plus, Blender is free, so why not figure it out?

Bryce was just simply gorgeous. Plus, technically I could design things in Ray Dream Designer and bring those objects into Bryce and put them inside of, you know, a real world looking place. Yeah, I miss this kind of creation. I should definitely get back into it.

But this is what I came up with back then, exploring with building worlds, building symbols, creating spaces that I'd like to inhabit in a fantastical way, obviously. Imagined worlds. Is that not something most people do? Maybe that's just a dreamer type thing, but it's kinda cool when you have the ability to actually produce some variation of what it is that you'd like to be in. And I gotta say, after all this time, I think these things have aged really well.
